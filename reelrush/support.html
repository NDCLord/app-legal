FAQ

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app > your name > Subscriptions, then choose ReelRush. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

An episode will not play.

Episodes stream over the internet. Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data, then close and reopen the episode.

Will my library move to a new phone?

Your library and viewing history are saved only on this phone. Your subscription comes back on a new phone with Restore purchases.

How do I turn reminders off?

Open the iOS Settings app > Notifications > ReelRush.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
