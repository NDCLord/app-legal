Privacy Policy

ReelRush · updated November 11, 2026

ReelRush plays short drama series in full-screen vertical video. There is no account and no sign-in. This page says plainly what the app touches and what it does not.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Your library, queue and saved series
  • Your viewing history and where you stopped in each episode
  • Genres you picked, caption settings and reminder settings
  • Your purchase status (cached on this phone)

Data that is sent off your phone

No personal profile. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or photos. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking tools in the app, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Two kinds of requests do leave your phone:

  • Video streaming. Episodes and their captions are streamed over the internet from a video host. Like any web request, the host sees your IP address and basic request details (such as the time and the file asked for) so it can deliver the video. The host may keep these technical access logs for up to 30 days for security and to keep the service running; they are not linked to an account.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple; we never see your card details. To check which plan is active on your device, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID created on your phone, your purchase receipts and transaction history from Apple, and basic device information (such as the iOS version and your IP address). It is used only to confirm and restore your purchases, not for advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example the next episode of a series you are watching) are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the iOS Settings app > Notifications > ReelRush.

Account

There is no account and no sign-in. No email or name is collected.

Children

ReelRush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on this phone. We hold no account data on a server. To ask RevenueCat to delete the purchase records linked to your app user ID, or for any other question, email us.

Contact

Questions or data requests: vlkduy@gmail.com. We reply within 5 business days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
