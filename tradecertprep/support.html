How to use it

  • Set your exam date, then practice a short set of questions each day.
  • Take a timed mock exam when you feel ready; your readiness score shows where to focus.
  • Missed questions come back until you get them right.

Common questions

I did not get a reminder. Allow notifications in iOS Settings > Trade Cert Prep, and check Focus mode.

I bought access on another phone. Tap Restore in Settings, using the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel? If your option renews, cancel in iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. One-time and fixed-length options do not renew.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is this an official exam? No. Trade Cert Prep is a practice tool. It is not affiliated with any licensing board, and a high score does not guarantee a pass.
