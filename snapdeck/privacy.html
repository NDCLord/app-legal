Privacy Policy

SnapDeck · updated November 8, 2026

SnapDeck turns a photo of your notes into flashcards. This page says plainly what the app touches and what it does not.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Your decks and cards (stored in a local database on the phone)
  • Study progress, reminder times and app settings
  • Your purchase status (cached from Apple)

Data that is sent off your phone

Nothing. In this version the cards are made on your phone; your photos, decks and cards are not sent to us or to any other service. There are no analytics, ads or tracking tools.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you tap Snap. The photo library opens only when you pick an existing photo; only that photo is used. iOS asks your permission first.

AI service

This version does not use an outside AI service. If a future version does, we will update this page first and name the service.

Purchases

SnapDeck Pro is an optional purchase through Apple. Prices come only from the App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

Account

There is no account and no sign-in. No email or name is collected.

Children

SnapDeck is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on this phone. Because we hold none of your data on a server, there is nothing for us to export or erase; email us if you have any question.

Contact

Questions or data requests: vlkduy@gmail.com. We reply within 5 business days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
