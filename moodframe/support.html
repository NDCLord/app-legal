Support

MoodFrame · updated October 17, 2026

Support page for MoodFrame, a private photo mood journal with small self-care guides.

Need help?

Email vlkduy@gmail.com. To get a fast answer, please tell us your iPhone model (for example iPhone 15), your iOS version, the app version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. Please do not send us your journal entries or photos.

Common questions

  • Is my journal sent anywhere?
    No. Check-ins, notes and photos stay on your phone. See the privacy policy.
  • Does MoodFrame read my mood from my photos?
    No. You choose the feeling yourself. Photos are only kept as a memory with your entry.
  • The app can't see my photos or camera.
    Open the iOS Settings app > MoodFrame and allow Photos or Camera. MoodFrame only uses them when you add a photo.
  • Will my journal move to a new phone?
    MoodFrame has no account or cloud sync of its own, so we can't move your journal for you.
  • How do I delete everything?
    In the app, open Account and tap "Delete all my data". This cannot be undone. Original photos in your Photos app are kept.
  • The guides didn't update.
    Guides refresh when you're online. Offline, the guides built into the app still work.
  • Is MoodFrame a medical app?
    No. It is a journal and does not diagnose or treat anything. If you need to talk to someone now, findahelpline.com lists free helplines in your country.
