Privacy Policy

MoodFrame · updated October 17, 2026

MoodFrame is a private photo mood journal built to keep your entries on your phone. This page says plainly what the app touches and what it does not.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Your check-ins: the feeling you choose, context and notes
  • Copies of photos you attach to a check-in
  • Care points and reading progress
  • App settings

These are saved in the app's storage on this device. We do not read, copy or send them anywhere. Your original photos in the Photos app are never changed. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Data that is sent off your phone

None of your journal. The app does not send your check-ins, notes, photos or mood choices to our servers or to any third party. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Guide downloads

To keep the self-care guides fresh, the app downloads the guide list and guide images from our content server at jpecom.com. These requests carry no cookies, no sign-in and none of your journal data. Like any web request, the server sees your IP address and basic request details (such as the time and the file asked for). The host may keep these technical access logs for up to 30 days for security and to keep the service running; they are not linked to your journal or to an account. If the server can't be reached, the guides built into the app are used.

Camera, photos and motion

  • The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to add a photo. iOS asks your permission first.
  • The motion sensor is used only to gently tilt the on-screen illustration. Motion readings are not saved.

Health information

Mood entries can be sensitive. They never leave your phone through the app. MoodFrame does not use Apple Health, and it never guesses your mood from photos. It is not a medical app.

Links to other sites

Some guides link to outside sources (for example the WHO or the NHS). These open in your browser, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

Account

There is no account and no sign-in. No email or name is collected.

Children

MoodFrame is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

  • Delete everything on this phone from Account > "Delete all my data". This removes your journal, care points, reading progress and the app's photo copies. Original photos are kept.
  • Ask us what data we hold about you (for this app: none beyond normal server logs).
  • Ask us to delete any data you sent us by email.

Contact

Questions or data requests: vlkduy@gmail.com. We reply within 5 business days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts sending any of your data off your phone, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.
