Privacy Policy

Whisper · updated November 8, 2026

Whisper is a calm, private app from the QuietSuite family. Everything you write or save stays on your phone. This page says plainly what the app touches and what it does not.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Your entries and saved items
  • Reminder times and app settings
  • Your purchase status (cached from Apple)

Data that is sent off your phone

Nothing you write. The app has no server and no account, and it makes no network requests of its own. Any AI features run on your iPhone with Apple's on-device model. There are no analytics, ads or tracking tools.

Camera and speech

Whisper does not use the camera or speech recognition. iOS may show these permission texts because a shared on-device system module requires them.

Widgets

If you add a Whisper widget, the app shares only today's line, its topic and your widget colours with it, through a private app group on this phone only.

Purchases

Whisper offers optional purchases through Apple. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

Account

There is no account and no sign-in. No email or name is collected.

Children

Whisper is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on this phone. Because we hold none of your data on a server, there is nothing for us to export or erase; email us if you have any question.

Contact

Questions or data requests: vlkduy@gmail.com. We reply within 5 business days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
