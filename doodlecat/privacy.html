Privacy Policy

DoodleCat · updated November 8, 2026

DoodleCat is a drawing app for children with a separate area for parents. It has no ads, no accounts and no sign-in. This page explains what the app keeps and what it never sends.

What stays on your phone

  • Your child's drawings, Studio pictures, stars, finished lessons and streaks.
  • Settings such as sound and the age band you choose.
  • Parent reminders you set and the weekly plan.
  • A small local count of app events (for example "lesson finished") so the parent area can show a summary. It is capped at 2,000 entries and never leaves the device.

What is sent off your phone

Nothing. DoodleCat makes no network calls. We run no servers, use no analytics or advertising services, and do not track your child across apps or websites.

Apple Intelligence (parent area only)

On devices that support it, the parent area can suggest the next lesson or write a short caption or weekly summary. This uses Apple's on-device model; the text is created on your phone and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Children never use this feature directly.

Backups

In the parent area you can export a backup file of drawings and progress and import it later. The file is created on your phone and goes only where you save or share it.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. Turning them off or denying permission does not affect drawing.

Sharing and printing

Sharing a drawing or printing coloring pages is behind the parent check. The file goes only where you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Purchases

Version 1.0 has no purchases. If a "DoodleCat Family" plan is added later, payment will be handled entirely by Apple; we will never see your card details, and this page will be updated first.

Children

DoodleCat is made for children. We collect no personal information from them. Everything for grown-ups (settings, reminders, sharing, printing, links) sits behind a parent check: a two-digit multiplication question.

Your choices and deleting data

All data lives on the device. To remove it, delete the app; iOS removes its data with it.
