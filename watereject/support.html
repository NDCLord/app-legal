Water Eject · updated November 18, 2026

How to use it

  • Turn the volume up and hold the phone with the speaker facing down.
  • Tap Clear my speaker. A 165 Hz tone plays for 20 seconds.
  • Open the Check tab to play the left and right speaker and say what you heard, or record 3 seconds to check the microphone.

Common questions

The sound is still muffled. Run one more cycle, then let the phone dry for a while. Results vary by device. If the speaker stays quiet, contact Apple or your device maker.

The microphone check does nothing. Allow microphone access in iOS Settings > Water Eject.

How many free cycles do I get? One free cycle every 24 hours. Water Eject Pro unlocks unlimited cycles.

Is Pro a subscription? No. It is a one-time purchase: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.

I bought Pro on another phone. Tap Restore in the paywall or in Settings, using the same Apple ID.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
