Support

Daybreak · updated November 8, 2026

Support page for Daybreak, a calm morning ritual app with breathing, check-ins and guided routines.

Need help?

Email vlkduy@gmail.com. To get a fast answer, please tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version, the app version (shown in the feedback email subject), and what you were doing when the problem happened. Please do not send us your journal entries.

Common questions

  • Is anything I write or say uploaded?
    No. Check-ins, journal lines and voice check-ins stay on your phone. See the privacy policy.
  • Voice check-in doesn't work.
    Open iOS Settings > Daybreak and allow Microphone and Speech Recognition. Daybreak needs Apple's on-device speech model for your language; if it is not installed, voice check-in stays off and you can type instead.
  • I don't get my morning reminder.
    Open iOS Settings > Daybreak > Notifications and allow them, then set the reminder time again in the app. Focus modes can also hide reminders.
  • I don't see the Apple Intelligence chip.
    It only shows on iPhones with Apple Intelligence turned on and set to English. Everything else in Daybreak works without it.
  • How do I move to a new phone?
    Daybreak has no account or cloud sync. Export a backup in the app, move the file to the new phone (for example with AirDrop or Files), then import it there.
  • How do I cancel Daybreak Unlimited?
    Open iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Daybreak, or use "Manage subscription" in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • I paid but Unlimited isn't unlocked.
    Open the paywall and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.
  • Is Daybreak a medical app?
    No. It is a wellness routine and does not diagnose or treat anything. If you need to talk to someone now, findahelpline.com lists free helplines in your country.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
