Privacy Policy

Daybreak · updated November 8, 2026

Daybreak is a calm morning ritual app: short breathing, check-ins and guided routines. It is built to keep what you write and say on your phone. This page says plainly what the app touches and what it does not.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Your check-ins, journal lines and saved lines
  • Your rituals, streak, goals and history
  • Reminder times and app settings
  • Simple usage counts (for example "a ritual finished today") used only to show your own progress

These are saved in the app's storage on this device (and in a shared app group so the widget and Live Activity can show today's ritual). We do not read, copy or send them anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Data that is sent off your phone

None. Daybreak has no account, no server of its own, and no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Voice check-ins

If you choose to record a voice check-in, the microphone is used only while you record. Your words are turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on your phone (the app requires on-device recognition and will not send audio to a server). The recording is deleted right after it becomes text. iOS asks your permission first.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, Daybreak can write short openers and recaps using Apple's on-device model. This runs on your phone; Daybreak does not send your text to us or to any other company. On other phones the app uses built-in text instead.

Notifications, widget and Live Activity

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. Daybreak does not use push notifications from a server.

Backups

You can export a backup file of your Daybreak data and import it later. The file is only saved or shared where you choose (for example Files or AirDrop). We never receive it.

Purchases

Daybreak Unlimited is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple ID details. The app only learns whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Feedback email

If you tap "Send feedback", your mail app opens a new email to us with the app version in the subject. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Health information

Daybreak is a wellness app, not a medical app. It does not use Apple Health and does not diagnose or treat anything.

Account

There is no account and no sign-in. No email or name is collected.

Children

Daybreak is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

  • All your Daybreak data is on your phone. Delete the app to delete it, or export a backup first if you want to keep it.
  • Ask us what data we hold about you (for this app: none).
  • Ask us to delete any data you sent us by email.

Contact

Questions or data requests: vlkduy@gmail.com. We reply within 5 business days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts sending any of your data off your phone, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.
