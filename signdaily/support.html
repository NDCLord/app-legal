How to use it

  • Open Today and start the lesson: watch a short video of a real signer.
  • Pick what the sign means, then sign it yourself along with the video and rate how it went.
  • Signs come back for review at the right time. Up to three new signs a day, plus quick reviews.

Common questions

I missed a day. That is fine. Streak freezes cover missed days, and your progress is never lost.

What is free? Units 1 and 2. Sign Daily Pro, an optional yearly subscription, unlocks unit 3 and beyond.

How do I cancel Pro? In iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

I bought Pro on another phone. Tap Restore in Settings or on the Pro screen, using the same Apple ID.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does this certify my ASL? No. Sign Daily is a practice tool. It does not replace learning from Deaf teachers and the Deaf community.
