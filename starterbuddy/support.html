Starter Buddy · updated November 18, 2026

How to use it

  • Add your starter and its feed ratio.
  • Log each feed with one tap on Today; the next feed shows there.
  • Mark how high it rose to spot its peak, and use the calculator for flour and water.

Common questions

I did not get a reminder. Allow notifications in iOS Settings > Starter Buddy, and check Focus mode.

What is free? One starter with recent history. Starter Buddy Pro unlocks unlimited starters and full rise history.

Is Pro a subscription? No. It is a one-time purchase: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.

I bought Pro on another phone. Tap Restore in Settings, using the same Apple ID.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
