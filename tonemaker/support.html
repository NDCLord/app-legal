How to use it

  • Tap record, or import audio from your files.
  • Trim the part you want and save it as a ringtone.
  • Follow the in-app steps to set it as your ringtone in iOS Settings.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? In iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

I bought on another phone. Tap Restore in the app, using the same Apple ID.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my recordings? Only on your phone; we never receive them.
