Privacy Policy

Tone Maker · updated November 19, 2026

Tone Maker (bundle com.jadephan.tonemaker) lets you record or pick audio and turn it into ringtones. This page says plainly what the app touches and what it does not.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Recordings, imported audio and the ringtones you create
  • Your app settings and trial reminder choice
  • If you choose Sign in with Apple: the ID (and name/email if you share them) that Apple gives the app, kept on this device only

These are saved in the app's storage on this device. We do not read, copy or send them anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Data that is sent off your phone

None. Tone Maker has no server of its own and no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We do not track you across other apps or websites and we do not collect personal data.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you tap record. Audio is processed and stored on your phone and is never uploaded.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

Sign-in is handled by Apple; the app keeps only the result on this phone. You can sign out in the app at any time.

Purchases

Optional purchases and subscriptions go through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple ID details. The app only learns whether your purchase is active. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, and use Restore in the app to unlock again on a new phone.

Notifications

If you allow them, Tone Maker schedules local notifications on your phone (for example a reminder before a trial ends). They are not sent from a server.

Children

Tone Maker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

  • All your Tone Maker data is on your phone. Delete the app to delete it.
  • Ask us what data we hold about you (for this app: none).

Contact

Questions or data requests: vlkduy@gmail.com. We reply within 5 business days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever sends data off your phone, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.
