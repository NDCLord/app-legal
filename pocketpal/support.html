Support

Pango · updated October 17, 2026

Support page for Pango, a tiny pocket pal for small self-care goals.

Need help?

Email vlkduy@gmail.com. To get a fast answer, please tell us your iPhone model (for example iPhone 15), your iOS version, the app version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

  • Is my data sent anywhere?
    No. Your pal, goals and reflections stay on your phone. See the privacy policy.
  • I bought Plus. How do I get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling?
    Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap "restore purchases" on the Plus screen or at the bottom of the home screen.
  • How do I cancel my subscription?
    Open the iOS Settings app > your name > Subscriptions, then choose Pango. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Your pal and everything it learned stay with you.
  • Will my pal move to a new phone?
    Not at the moment. Your pal is saved only on this phone and is not included in iCloud or computer backups, and Pango has no account or cloud sync, so a new phone starts with a new egg. Purchases can always be restored.
  • How do I turn reminders off?
    Open the iOS Settings app > Notifications > Pango.
  • How do I erase everything?
    In the app, open settings and tap "erase pango and all local data". This cannot be undone. Plus stays on your Apple Account.
  • I'm having a hard time. Can Pango help?
    Pango is a pet, not a counselor. If you need to talk to someone now, findahelpline.com lists free helplines in your country.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refund requests go through reportaproblem.apple.com. If a feature you bought does not open, email us and we will get it working.
