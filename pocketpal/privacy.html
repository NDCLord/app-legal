Privacy Policy

Pango · updated October 17, 2026

Pango is built to keep your data on your phone. This page says plainly what the app touches and what it does not.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Your pal's name and progress
  • Your goals, check-ins and reflections
  • Postcards, acorns and shop items
  • Reminder and app settings

These are saved in the app's storage on this device. We do not read, copy or send them anywhere. There is no account and no sign-in. Deleting the app deletes them.

Data that is sent off your phone

None. The app does not send your personal data to our servers or to any third party. We have no server for this app. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Reminders

Gentle reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. iOS asks your permission first, and you can turn them off at any time in the iOS Settings app. We do not use push notifications.

In-app purchases

All purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never receive or store your card details; the app only learns from Apple which plan is active, to unlock the matching items. You can manage or cancel a subscription in the iOS Settings app (your name > Subscriptions).

Links to other sites

Some buttons open a web page in Safari, for example a free list of helplines, Apple's subscription page or this page. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Pango is a pet, not a counselor, and is not a medical app.

Children

Pango is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Crash reports from Apple

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They do not include your goals, reflections or pal.

Your rights

  • Erase your pal and all app data on this phone from Settings > "erase pango and all local data".
  • Ask us what data we hold about you (for this app: none).
  • Ask us to delete any data you sent us by email.

Contact

Questions or data requests: vlkduy@gmail.com. We reply within 5 business days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts sending any data off your phone, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated before that version is released.
